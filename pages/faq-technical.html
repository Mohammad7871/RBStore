Technical Support — Frequently Asked Questions

How does RB Store manage hospital warranties?

All products from authorized brands include manufacturer-backed warranties. For institutional purchases, RB Store issues a procurement snapshot at the time of quote which includes serial numbers, batch references, and warranty duration. Our after-sales team coordinates warranty claims directly with OEMs and provides onsite support where contracts include installation and service.

What are typical bulk delivery times for hotel projects?

Delivery times depend on product availability, quantity, and destination city. For stocked items within Pakistan, typical lead time is 3–10 business days for major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad). For large-scale projects (100+ units) or items requiring import, lead times may range from 4–12 weeks. RB Store provides transparent ETA during quote and offers staged deliveries for project rollouts.

Can RB Store handle installation and commissioning?

Yes. We offer optional installation and commissioning packages for medical equipment and hospitality fixtures. These are quoted separately during the enterprise inquiry process and include certified technician labor, onsite testing, and handover documentation.

How are spare parts and consumables handled?

Spare parts are catalogued as separate SKUs. For critical hospital equipment we maintain spares inventory for supported OEMs. Consumables are available on recurring supply contracts and can be scheduled for periodic delivery.

What quality checks are performed before dispatch?

Every shipment goes through a quality control checklist: visual inspection, functionality test (where applicable), serial number verification, and packaging validation. For high-value or sensitive medical devices, we perform factory acceptance tests where required.

How does RB Store support regulatory compliance?

We ensure all medical devices comply with relevant Pakistani regulations and OEM certifications. Documentation and certificates are provided at the time of delivery and are kept on-file for audit purposes.

How do I request a custom installation quote?

Use the "Request Bulk Quote" flow on any product card, provide project quantity and site details, and our projects team will follow up with an onsite assessment and formal proposal.
