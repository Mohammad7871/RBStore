40 Years of Legacy

Built in the Forge of Practical Engineering

RB Store has served institutional and commercial clients for four decades. From the earliest days repairing and refurbishing medical equipment, to scaling national distribution channels for international brands, our journey combines hands-on technical mastery with enterprise-grade logistics. Today, RB Store Pakistan operates as the Master Authorized Distributor for leading brands, delivering quality, warranty, and institutional procurement excellence across hospitals and hotels.

Our Evolution

  1. 1986–1998: Technical service and local repairs — building trust with clinics and hotels.
  2. 1999–2010: Regional distribution, formal partnerships with international OEMs.
  3. 2011–2020: National logistics, warranty, and after-sales infrastructure.
  4. 2021–Present: RB Store Pakistan — procurement portals, B2B platforms, and enterprise-grade quoting.
