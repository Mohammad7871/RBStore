RB STORE PAKISTAN NAMED PRIMARY PROCUREMENT PARTNER FOR MEDICAL AND HOSPITALITY SECTORS

Islamabad, Pakistan — May 20, 2026 — RB Store Pakistan (Pvt.) Ltd., a leading enterprise procurement and distribution company, today announced that it has expanded its role as the primary supplier of medical and hospitality equipment across Pakistan. Catering to hospitals, clinics, and hotel chains, RB Store delivers verified OEM products, comprehensive warranty coverage, and institutional-grade logistics.

With an enterprise-grade catalog approaching one million items accessible through a modular JSON-driven procurement portal, RB Store provides fast catalog indexing, dedicated enterprise inquiry workflows, and real-time market valuation requests tailored for bulk buyers.

“Our mission is to bring trust and predictability to large-scale institutional procurement in Pakistan,” said CEO, RB Store Pakistan. “By replacing open retail pricing with a market valuation workflow and delivering a premium procurement experience, we empower procurement officers and facility managers to make informed decisions at scale.”

Key highlights:

- Master Authorized Distributor relationships with leading brands across medical and hospitality categories.
- Modular data approach optimized for low-latency browsing of very large catalogs.
- Enterprise inquiry pipeline for real-time market valuations and bulk-quote workflows.
- National logistics and dedicated after-sales support for project deployments.
