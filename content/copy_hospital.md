ICU Bed — Technical Description (Clinical, Trustworthy, Superior)

The RB Premium ICU Bed Series is engineered for critical-care environments, offering precision-adjustable ergonomics and medical-grade durability. Constructed from 316L stainless-steel framing with an antimicrobial powder coat, the bed supports dynamic positioning across head, knee, and Trendelenburg profiles via brushless DC actuators rated for continuous duty. Integrated side-rails provide patient restraint and rapid-release egress. The mattress platform accepts standard pressure-relief surfaces and includes a central drainage channel for infection control. Electrical subsystems are protected to IEC 60601-1 standards and include battery-backed control for uninterrupted operation during transfers.

Key Specifications:

- Frame Material: 316L stainless steel, antimicrobial finish
- Actuators: 24V brushless DC, continuous duty, IP42
- Load Capacity: 300 kg (safe working load)
- Compliance: IEC 60601-1 electrical safety; ISO 13485 manufacturing controls
- Infection Control: Smooth radiused surfaces, removable trays, central drainage

Oxygen Concentrator — Technical Description (Clinical, Trustworthy, Superior)

The RB Medical Oxygen Concentrator line delivers continuous-flow oxygen with high purity suitable for clinical and ward applications. Featuring PSA (pressure swing adsorption) technology with medical-grade molecular sieves, the unit maintains oxygen concentration stability across a wide flow range. Redundant filtration protects the compressor and ensures particulate-free delivery. Control systems provide real-time oxygen purity readouts and alarm notifications compliant with EN ISO 80601-2-69.

Key Specifications:

- Flow Range: 1–10 L/min (continuous models) with ±1% stability
- Purity: ≥ 93% ±3% at rated flow
- Noise Level: < 48 dB(A)
- Standards: EN ISO 80601-2-69, CE marked
- Maintenance: Modular sieve cartridges, tool-less access panels

Complete Surgical Set — Technical Description (Clinical, Trustworthy, Superior)

RB Surgical Sets are curated instrument suites intended for general and specialized surgical procedures. Instruments are manufactured from surgical-grade stainless steel (AISI 440/420) with precision-ground cutting edges and electropolished surfaces for corrosion resistance. Each set includes sterilization trays and instrument-level inventory with QR-coded identifiers for traceability and sterilization logs. Packaging conforms to ISO 11607 for sterile barrier systems.

Key Specifications:

- Material: AISI 440/420 surgical stainless steel, electropolished
- Traceability: QR-coded instrument IDs linked to sterilization records
- Packaging: ISO 11607-compliant sterilization trays and pouches
- Warranty: Manufacturing defects 3 years; regrinding service available
